# Free 14-day trial

## No credit card

Start your PLI Cloud trial without entering a card. 14 full days, all P0 + premium features (OCR attachments, unlimited accounts).

## What happens after

- **D-3**: email reminder (never push, not intrusive)
- **D-1**: last reminder with CTA "Continue with PLI Plus"
- **D+0 (trial end)**: PLI goes read-only + upgrade CTA. **No data erased during 30 days.**
- **D+30**: if still no upgrade, your Cloud data is purged (x2 email notices beforehand)

## If you upgrade

No interruption. You keep everything. Upgrade day becomes your billing day (not retroactive).

## Coming back later

Your account stays, your settings too. OAuth reconnect is enough to resume. Purged data (after D+30) doesn't return, but emails at Gmail/Microsoft are intact — sync reimports them.

## Cancel the trial

Do nothing. There's no "Cancel" — nothing to cancel (no card).

## Does the trial include PLI Local?

No. PLI Local is the license offer (49 € or 5 €/month). The trial covers PLI Cloud only. You can switch from Cloud trial to Local license without loss.
