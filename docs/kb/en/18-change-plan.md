# Changing plan

## From Cloud to Plus (Local)

Settings → **Subscription** → "Switch to PLI Plus". Pick perpetual (49 €) or monthly (5 €). Stripe payment. License delivered by email within 2 min and activated automatically on the installed desktop app.

Your Cloud account stays active until the end of the paid period (or trial). You can use both temporarily.

## From Plus to Cloud

Settings → **Subscription** → "Add PLI Cloud". You pay 7 €/month (or 70 €/year). Sync turns on, your mail accounts are accessible across devices.

Locally-stored data **stays local** — it is not auto-uploaded to Cloud. To migrate, see [GDPR export](24-gdpr-export.md), then import into Cloud.

## Monthly → Annual (or vice versa)

Settings → **Subscription** → "Change periodicity". The change kicks in at the next billing date. You lose nothing in-flight.

## Temporary pause

No official pause. Cancel, and come back whenever — see [Cancel](19-cancel.md).

## Billing question

[Support](30-support.md) replies within 24 h on weekdays.
