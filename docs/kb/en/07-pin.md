# Pinning important messages

A pinned message appears at the top of the **Pinned** tab and can be recalled even if archived.

## Pin

- **Keyboard**: `P` on the open message or in the list
- **Mouse**: right-click → Pin
- **Swipe**: not mapped to pin (swipe does archive/snooze, see [Swipe](14-swipe.md))

## Unpin

Same shortcut `P`.

## Visual cue

A small gold pin icon to the right of the sender, in the list. The **Pinned** tab in the left rail shows the count.

## Limit

Soft cap of **50 pins** per account (a banner suggests cleanup above that). No hard cap.

## Versus Gmail stars / Outlook flags

Pins are **PLI-local** by default (not synced back to Gmail stars or Outlook flags) to avoid polluting your native client. Toggle sync in Settings → Accounts → **⋮ → Sync pins to [provider]** if you want.

## Smart expiry (V1.1+)

Opt-in: a pin auto-expires if the conversation gets no reply for 30 days (prevents stale piles). Settings → Preferences → **Auto-clean pins**.
