# Invoices

## Where to find them

Settings → **Subscription** → **Billing history**. All issued invoices, with a **Download PDF** button per line.

## Format

A4 PDF, English or French (based on account locale), legal mentions of JMJ Consulting (HQ, RCS, EU VAT ID). Compliant with EU electronic invoice standards.

## Received by email

Each Stripe payment sends an email with PDF attached (to the primary PLI account email). If you don't get it → check spam → otherwise [support](30-support.md).

## Edit billing details (company name, address, VAT)

Settings → **Billing** → **Details**. You can switch between "Individual" and "Company" (and enter an EU intra-community VAT number, which applies reverse-charge if you're in the EU outside France).

Changes apply to **future** invoices, not past ones (accounting principle).

## Rectify a past invoice

Wrong details → [support](30-support.md) → credit note + new invoice issued within 48 working hours.

## Payment declined

Stripe retries automatically within 3-5 days. In the meantime, PLI stays active (7-day grace period). You'll get an email to update your card.

After 7 days without payment → read-only suspension (like a cancellation), immediate reactivation on payment.
