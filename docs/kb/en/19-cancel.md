# Cancelling a subscription

## PLI Cloud

Settings → **Subscription** → "Cancel subscription". Confirmation asked (checkbox + click). No justification required.

- Subscription stays **active until the end of the paid period** (access until then).
- No future charges.
- Data turns **read-only** at period end, then purged 30 days later (x2 email notices).

## PLI Plus (Monthly subscription)

Same flow. License stays valid until the end of the paid month, then the app switches to **essentials free mode** (read-only, no new sync, no incoming attachments).

## PLI Plus (Perpetual license)

Nothing to cancel: you bought it once, it's yours. Free updates stop 12 months after purchase. To extend, buy a 12-month extension (30 €) or switch to subscription.

## Refund

- **Trial**: no refund (it's free).
- **Cloud / Plus subscription**: pro-rata refund **within 14 days** of first payment (EU, consumer protection). After 14 days, no refund but you can cancel.
- **Plus perpetual**: full refund **within 14 days** if the app hasn't been used more than 10 times. After, case-by-case ([support](30-support.md)).

## Recover your data before cancelling

Do a full [GDPR export](24-gdpr-export.md). Recommended, not required.

## Coming back later?

Your account stays (email + settings). Just resubscribe — we reimport everything still present at Gmail/Microsoft.
