# Plans and pricing

## PLI Cloud

- **Monthly**: 7 €/month
- **Annual**: 70 €/year (2 months free)

Included: unlimited mail accounts, attachment OCR, encryption at rest, GDPR export, email support.

## PLI Plus (Local)

- **Perpetual license**: 49 € (12 months of updates included)
- **Subscription**: 5 €/month (updates for life while active)

Included: everything in Local mode + dedicated decryption key + advanced import/export tools.

## Beta pricing

During the beta (M4), **everything is free**. Beta users get **-50% lifetime** on the plan of their choice at M5 commercial launch.

## Billing

Stripe direct debit (card). VAT included (20 % EU). PDF invoice auto-emailed after each payment.
