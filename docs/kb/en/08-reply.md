# Replying to a message

## Quick reply

Open a conversation → type directly in the bottom bar → `Cmd/Ctrl + Enter` to send.

## Full reply

`R` on an open message → full composer opens with history quoted.

## Reply All

`Shift + R`. PLI warns you if there are more than **8 recipients** (accidental "reply all" safeguard).

## Forward

`F`. Subject auto-prefixed `Fwd:`.

## Canned replies

Type `/` in the composer → pick a snippet. Edit your snippets in Settings → **Canned replies**. Variables: `{{firstname}}`, `{{me}}`, `{{date}}`.

## Draft saves

Every 3 seconds (or on blur). Visible in **Drafts** tab. No data loss if you close by accident.

## Scheduled send

Click the arrow next to **Send** → **Schedule send** → pick date/time. Stored locally (Plus) or on our servers (Cloud, AES-256).

## Undo send

30 seconds after send: bottom toast **"Sent · Undo"**. Actually holds the email in the outbox before dispatching — not a fake undo.
