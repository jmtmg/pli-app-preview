# Delete my account

## Effect

**Final and irreversible** deletion of your PLI account:

- All your data (emails, attachments, contacts, notes, preferences) erased from our servers within 30 days
- OAuth refresh tokens revoked immediately on the PLI side
- Email removed from communication lists (trial nurturing, newsletter)
- Invoice(s) kept 10 years (French accounting obligation) in anonymized form (name/email pseudonymized, only the amount and VAT useful)

## Before deleting

You **may** (not required):

1. [Export your data](24-gdpr-export.md)
2. Manually revoke OAuth access at Google/Microsoft as a double safety

## How

Settings → **Privacy** → **Delete my account**.

- Type the keyword `DELETE` to confirm (anti-accident click)
- Confirmation email sent with click-link within 48 h (else action cancelled)
- Once confirmed: instant sign-out, the app won't reopen

## And my emails at Gmail / Microsoft?

**Untouched.** PLI does not have the power (or the desire) to delete emails from your origin account. Deleting your PLI account only erases what we had on our side.

## Grace period

- **D+0 to D+7**: you can undo the deletion by signing in (reactivation link in the last sent email)
- **D+8 to D+30**: progressive purge
- **D+30**: full erase, no return

## Deletion certificate

On request ([support](30-support.md)), we issue a timestamped PDF certifying the deletion (useful if you need to prove it to a corporate DPO).
