# Getting started

Welcome to PLI. This guide gets you to your first useful action in under 3 minutes.

## Step 1 — Create your account

[app.pli.app/signup](https://app.pli.app/signup). Email + password. A magic link is sent — click it to confirm (valid 24 h).

## Step 2 — Pick your mode

- **Cloud** (recommended if you want multi-device): 14-day trial, no card needed. See [Cloud vs Local](05-local-vs-cloud.md).
- **Local** (100% on your device): requires PLI Plus license. See [Plans](17-plans.md).

## Step 3 — Connect your mailbox

Gmail → see [Connect Gmail](02-connect-gmail.md). Microsoft 365 / Outlook → see [Connect Microsoft](03-connect-microsoft.md). First sync runs in the background — browse PLI while it works.

## Step 4 — The 60-second product tour

On first open, a 5-step tour highlights key actions: inbox, filters, swipe, search, compose. You can skip it ([Product tour](04-product-tour.md)).

## Step 5 — First useful action

Open your inbox, pick one message from a real human (not a notification), and either **reply in 2 lines** or **archive with a right swipe**. That's it — you've used PLI.
