## PLI, a new French email client, opens its private beta: "putting human email back at the center"

**Paris, August 10, 2026** — JMJ Consulting announces today the opening of the **PLI private beta**, an alternative email client that separates human emails from automated notifications, runs either **100% local** or on **EU cloud**, and opens for commercial availability in October 2026.

### A product built for people who suffer their inbox

A knowledge worker receives on average **121 emails per day**[^1], and more than **60% are automated notifications** (order confirmations, newsletters, tool alerts). PLI bets that separating these flows gives users time and attention back.

> "We don't want just another email client. We want to separate what deserves a human reply from what only deserves to be skimmed when you have time. The distinction is visible from the first tab."
>
> — JM, founder of JMJ Consulting

### Privacy: two modes, one commitment

PLI offers two user-selectable modes:

- **PLI Cloud** — hosted in Germany (Hetzner Falkenstein), AES-256 encryption at rest, no transfer outside the EU. 7 €/month, 14-day trial, no card required.
- **PLI Plus (Local)** — desktop app, SQLite database with SQLCipher AES-256 encryption, no data leaves the device. 49 € perpetual license or 5 €/month.

In both cases, PLI does not sell advertising, does not profile users, and supports native GDPR export (Article 20) in one click.

### A 4-week private beta, 100 users

- **Dates**: August 12 → September 9, 2026
- **Batches**: 4 batches of 25 people (weekly invitations)
- **Access**: waitlist open at [pli.app/waitlist](https://pli.app/waitlist)
- **Beta commitment**: -50% for life on subscription for all beta users

### Commercial launch planned mid-October 2026

After the beta, PLI opens to the public. ProductHunt, Hacker News, and European Hunt-like communities are on the agenda. M4 pre-orders will be converted automatically.
